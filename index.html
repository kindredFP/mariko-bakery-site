Welcome to KO-BO

Baked slowly, with simple ingredients.

KO-BO BAKEHOUSE is a small-batch home bakery located in Mississauga. Every bake is prepared with homemade sourdough, simple ingredients, and lots of love & care.

Items are made to order, so each order is prepared after payment is confirmed. Please submit orders at least three days before your requested pickup date.
