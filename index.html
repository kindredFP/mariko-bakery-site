How to order

Made to order, with a little notice

Because every item is prepared after payment is confirmed, please allow enough time before your pickup date.

Choose your bake

Browse the available bakes and decide what you would like to order. Menu details and availability are to be confirmed.

Submit your order

Read all order details carefully and complete the required information before submitting your order.

Confirm & pick up

Orders are prepared after payment is confirmed, then ready for pickup at your chosen slot.

Important: All items are made to order and prepared after payment is confirmed. Orders must be submitted 3 days prior to the pickup date.

Place an order

Choose as many bakes as you like, set a quantity for each, then pick a pickup slot — it opens an email to Mariko with everything filled in. No payment on the site; orders are prepared after payment is confirmed by e-transfer.
